# EPIC-11 Milestone E — Operational Evidence & Economics

## Goal

The user can reconstruct what happened, why it happened, and what it cost.

## Scope

- logs
- events
- audit
- diagnostics
- readiness evidence
- economic visibility
- quote
- reservation
- metering
- settlement
- receipts

## Requests

- EV-01 Logs & Events
- EV-02 Audit Trail
- EV-03 Diagnostics & Evidence
- EV-04 Economic Overview
- EV-05 Quote & Reservation
- EV-06 Metering & Settlement

## Dependencies

- Operational Execution
- evidence and audit correlation
- economic contract exposure through Product API

## Success criteria

- operational history can be reconstructed from the surface
- evidence is correlated, redacted, and attributable
- economic state is visible without collapsing the control plane into a finance product
