## Canonical authority map

- EconomicService remains the durable authority for usage recording, settlement
  and receipt issuance.
- OperationalEvidenceService projects usage, settlement and receipt evidence
  into Product API-readable records.
- Tenant isolation remains authoritative through the economic service and
  Product API context.
- Execution/run correlation flows from usage through settlement and receipt
  records.
- Pricing provenance remains anchored in the existing quote/pricing authority.
- Dashboard usage/settlement panels are truthful, read-only projections.

## Canonical term coverage

The AEES slice preserves distinct semantics for:

- observed;
- recorded;
- pending settlement;
- settled;
- rejected;
- duplicate / idempotent replay;
- unavailable / unknown.

read-only summaries. Unsupported or unavailable values remain explicitly
unavailable; no fabricated histories or totals are introduced.
