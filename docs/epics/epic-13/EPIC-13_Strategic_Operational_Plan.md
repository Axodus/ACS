# EPIC-13 Strategic & Operational Plan

## Mission

Transform the Economics boundary from EPIC-12 into a governed planning frontier
for Billing & Financial Operations.

## Strategic problem

EPIC-12 deferred billing, invoices, payment rails, tenant billing, pricing,
receipts, settlement, reconciliation, and financial compliance. EPIC-13 exists
to define that boundary explicitly before implementation begins.

## Operational problem

Operators need clear contracts for billable events, payment states, invoice
artifacts, tenant responsibility, and auditability before any financial work
can be implemented safely.

## Why EPIC-13 exists after EPIC-12

EPIC-12 closed Economics as operational evidence. EPIC-13 is the next explicit
boundary where financial operations are planned, governed, and constrained.

## Organizing principle

```text
Financial Truth > Compliance > Tenant Accountability > Operational Control > UX
```

## Execution principles

- Fluxo > Módulo > Tela
- Product API is source of truth
- no simulated financial truth
- no payment without explicit approval
- no billing claim without evidence
- no production financial operation without gates

## Macro-phases

1. Billing Boundary & Financial Truth
2. Pricing, Quote & Invoice Contracts
3. Payment Rails Boundary
4. Tenant Billing & Account Responsibility
5. Receipts, Settlement & Reconciliation
6. Financial Audit, Compliance & Risk
7. Billing UX & Operator Acceptance
8. Final Hardening & Closure

## Success criteria

- planning package exists and is coherent
- financial claims remain not claimed
- scope boundaries are explicit
- future implementation has an unambiguous starting point

## Non-goals

- real payment capture
- real invoices
- tenant billing product implementation
- accounting integrations
- tax automation

## Planning output expectation

The next planning pass must produce the executive plan, boundary review,
candidate inventory, and milestone ordering without expanding scope into
implementation.
