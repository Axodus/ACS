## Specialist-review inventory

| Question | Specialist review needed |
| --- | --- |
| Who owns a Genome definition, modification, and generated derivative? | IP / contract counsel |
| Are prompts, models, tools, datasets, and provider outputs sublicensable? | IP, provider terms, data counsel |
| Does a token or transfer convey any license right in each jurisdiction? | contract, consumer, financial-regulatory counsel |
| Can rights be revoked, assigned, or expired? | contract and jurisdictional counsel |
| What liability attaches to harmful or infringing configuration? | product, liability, security counsel |
| What tax, sanctions, AML, intermediary, and marketplace duties apply? | tax, compliance, regulatory counsel |
| What privacy/data protection limits constrain Evidence and publication? | privacy/data-protection counsel |

## Research observations

Technical access, copyright/IP ownership, possession of a cryptographic asset, and authority to operate a canonical Agent are separate questions. Protected Genome components may be trade secrets; public commitments may reveal metadata; a right to instantiate may be narrower than a right to inspect, modify, publish, or commercialize.

This inventory is not legal advice and reaches no legal conclusion.
