```text
publication -> discovery -> evaluation -> license acquisition

```

## Options and constraints

| Topic | Options to investigate | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| discovery | catalog, private offer, curated listing | listing is not quality certification |
| primary sale | one-time, usage-based, subscription | pricing is a CEO decision |
| resale | prohibited, permitted, controlled | legal transfer and fraud controls |
| derivatives | permissive, restricted, reciprocal | derivative definition is unresolved |
| royalties | none, fixed, capped chain, negotiated | ancestor propagation remains OPEN |
| scarcity/supply | open, capped, editioned | scarcity is not technical quality |
| $Neurons | payment, access unit, accounting reference, none | role is OPEN; no integration implied |

## Derivative economics questions

For `Genome A -> Genome B -> Genome C`, source terms could permit instances yet restrict derivative publication; possibly preserve rights for ancestors; or permit independent commercialization. Every option faces rights provenance, independently convergent work, depth cap, conflicts, transfer, revocation, tax, and enforcement questions. Unbounded ancestor royalties are a material risk; a future model would require an explicit cap or other containment rule. No economic right is created by ancestry in this research.

Marketplace, royalties, pricing, settlement, and $Neurons mechanics require CEO authorization before CTO feasibility design.
