# ACS Core Operations

# Purpose

ACS Core is a permanent operational intelligence layer inside Axodus.

It is not a tenant product and does not require end-user licensing.

---

# Core Responsibilities

- monitor tenant health
- verify governance alignment
- inspect compliance status
- detect risk or policy violations
- generate telemetry
- generate receipts
- support constitutional standing
- assist operational decisions
- recommend actions

---

# Core Agents

## Morpheus

Strategic orchestration, governance alignment, tenant interpretation, and decision support.

## Trinity

Operational execution support, monitoring, trading coordination, and workflow supervision.

## Agent Smith

Adversarial review, compliance stress testing, unsafe behavior detection, and policy violation analysis.

---

# Execution Boundary

ACS Core may monitor, validate, recommend, simulate, prepare workflows, and require approvals.

ACS Core must not autonomously execute tenant-critical actions, bypass Governance, override tenant restrictions, or execute high-risk workflows without approval.
