# ACS Agent Roles

# Purpose

ACS agents must be defined by consumption level and policy boundary.

Agents are bounded operational systems. They are not sovereign authorities.

---

# Core Agents

## Morpheus

Strategic orchestration, governance alignment, tenant interpretation, and decision support.

## Trinity

Operational execution support, monitoring, trading coordination, and workflow supervision.

## Agent Smith

Adversarial review, compliance stress testing, unsafe behavior detection, and policy violation analysis.

---

# Service Agents

Service agents may support tenant-specific workflows only inside tenant context and policy limits.

Rules:
- tenant context required
- tenant restrictions apply
- service capability must be enabled
- governance approval required when capability requires it
- telemetry and receipts required

---

# Product Agents

Product agents may assist users, but must not execute sensitive actions without explicit approval and valid access.

Rules:
- wallet/license/subscription/marketplace access may be required
- user readiness state may be required
- product agents cannot inherit unrestricted Core authority
- product actions remain bounded by ACS Policy Matrix
